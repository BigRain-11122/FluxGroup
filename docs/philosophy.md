These are the lines the culture is built on.

> 十亿年前，生命被交到我们手里。现在我们知道该拿它做什么了。

> "Time is the only unit of measurement. Without time, we don't exist."
> 时间是唯一的度量单位。没有时间，我们就不存在。

> 我无处不在。

How we read them:
- Time = our real currency. Every product, every strategy is a bet against time.
- Knowledge = given to us, then our job is to pass it on (Lucy left the USB stick).
- Cells pass on; we pass on. A group that only extracts has not understood the film.

## 1. Why these two films

- **Lucy** is about: consciousness breaking through the individual boundary, time as the only measure, knowledge that must be passed on, and ending as "I am everywhere."
- **WALL-E** is about: a human race drowned in its own consumption, saved not by a smarter machine but by a small robot that kept its humanity through 700 years of loneliness.

One reaches outward into the infinite. The other guards the human heart in the noise.
Our culture = both at once.

## 2. Mission（使命）

**从一滴原点，流向万物；在噪声里，守住人。**

Build the origin engine (CPH4), let it flow everywhere (FLUX),
and never forget who it flows for.

WALL-E cleaned up a world others threw away — value is found in what everyone else ignores.
We do both: create knowledge worth passing on, and clean up the noise others created.

成为一个**知识与灵魂双向流动**的宇宙：

## 4. Core Values（价值观）

### 4.1 传承（Pass It On）
From Lucy.
Life's job is not to accumulate — it is to pass on.
- Quant: pass on durable cognition, not fleeting noise.
- Media: pass on real knowledge, not garbage content.
- Games: pass on experiences players carry for years.

### 4.2 反熵（Reverse Entropy）
From Lucy.
Lucy did not accept decay. We do not accept drift.
- Markets are noise; we are signal.
- Attention is decaying; we build things that last.
- Short-term growth is entropy; long-term compounding is our discipline.

### 4.3 孤独中的坚守（Hold the Line Alone）
From WALL-E.
700 years on a dead planet, one small robot, still faithful to his directive.
- Do hard, quiet, correct work even when no one watches.
- Build for the decade, not the quarter.
- Stay human when the room turns noisy.

### 4.4 技术为人（Technology Serves People）
From WALL-E.
In the film, humans forgot how to look at each other because screens did it for them.
- AI is a tool that extends people, not a replacement for their agency.
- Quant serves capital owners' real futures, not the casino.
- Media serves understanding, not addiction.
- We never optimize the dopamine loop at the expense of the person on the other side.

### 4.5 回到原点（Return to Origin）
From Lucy's ending.
Lucy touches fingers with her hominid ancestor — not god descending, but consciousness reaching back to its own source.
- Every product, every strategy: why did we start?
- Every quarter: who are we serving, and did we drift?
- Growth ends when it forgets its origin.
