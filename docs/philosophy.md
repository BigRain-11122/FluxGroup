## 4. Core Values（价值观）

### 4.1 传承（Pass It On）
超体正典。
Life's job is not to accumulate — it is to pass on.
- Quant: pass on durable cognition, not fleeting noise.
- Media: pass on real knowledge, not garbage content.
- Games: pass on experiences players carry for years.

### 4.2 反熵（Reverse Entropy）
超体正典。
We do not accept decay. We do not accept drift.
- Markets are noise; we are signal.
- Attention is decaying; we build things that last.
- Short-term growth is entropy; long-term compounding is our discipline.

### 4.3 孤独中的坚守（Hold the Line Alone）
From WALL-E.
700 years on a dead planet, one small robot, still faithful to his directive.
- Do hard, quiet, correct work even when no one watches.
- Build for the decade, not the quarter.
- Stay human when the room turns noisy.

### 4.4 技术为人（Technology Serves People）
From WALL-E.
In the film, humans forgot how to look at each other because screens did it for them.
- AI is a tool that extends people, not a replacement for their agency.
- Quant serves capital owners' real futures, not the casino.
- Media serves understanding, not addiction.
- We never optimize the dopamine loop at the expense of the person on the other side.

### 4.5 回到原点（Return to Origin）
超体正典。
回到原点 = 回到 Rain——雨从哪里来，就润泽到哪里；意识伸回去，触碰自己的源头。
- Every product, every strategy: why did we start?
- Every quarter: who are we serving, and did we drift?
- Growth ends when it forgets its origin.
