## 2. Meaning

### FLUX
- Physics: flux = flow through a surface.
- The group runs three flows at once:
  - data flow (AI)
  - capital flow (quant)
  - traffic flow (media)
- Short, hard, modern. No classical Chinese allusions.

### CPH4
- From the film Lucy: the substance that lets a brain reach 100%.
- In this brand: the origin substance, the AI engine, the explosion point.
- CPH4 Labs = the research core that builds the engine.

### 超体宇宙 (Chinese parent)
- 超体 = superbody / Lucy
- 宇宙 = universe
- Narrative: from one explosion, a universe of flow.

---

## 3. Core philosophy

Taken from Lucy:
- Time is the only unit of measure.
- Knowledge is unbounded.
- I am everywhere.

Brand promise: build the origin engine (CPH4), then let it flow everywhere (FLUX).

## 6. Visual rules

- Monochrome: black, white, one accent only.
- Typography: geometric sans-serif, large whitespace.
- Mood: restrained, technical, cold. Reference: OpenAI, Anthropic.
- Mark: a single point (CPH4) radiating into flowing lines (FLUX).
- No ornate / classical / Chinese-calligraphy style.

3. Must connect back to FLUX (flow) or CPH4 (origin/explosion).
